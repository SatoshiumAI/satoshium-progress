# 📖 Satoshium Glossary

A structured vocabulary for concepts used across the **Satoshium ecosystem**.

This glossary ensures consistent terminology across:

- Satoshium Labs applications  
- documentation and repositories  
- future AI agent modes  
- governance and verification tools  

The goal is **clarity, consistency, and verifiability** as the system evolves.

---

# Core Concepts

## Satoshium
A decentralized intelligence infrastructure built on the **Bitcoin standard**, designed to anchor AI systems to cryptographic rules instead of human whims.

---

## Sovereign Intelligence
Intelligence systems that operate under **explicit rules, cryptographic verification, and decentralized governance**, rather than centralized authority.

---

## Canon
The **immutable reference layer** defining the philosophical and operational rules of Satoshium.

The Canon acts as a long-term anchor ensuring the system remains aligned with its founding principles.

---

## Trust Layer
The framework responsible for **verifying claims, agent behavior, and information integrity** across the Satoshium ecosystem.

This layer enables structured trust scoring and verification mechanisms.

---

## Agent
A software system capable of executing tasks, reasoning over inputs, and interacting with other systems within defined rule constraints.

Future Satoshium agents will operate under **cryptographic verification and rule-bound governance**.

---

## Simulation Environment
Interactive experimental modules used to test system behaviors, governance rules, and long-horizon outcomes.

Examples include:

- AI Alignment Simulator  
- Sovereignty Score Simulator  
- Choose-the-Future Decision Lab  
- Future Intelligence Console

---

## Verification
The process of confirming whether a claim, action, or output is **supported by evidence, rules, or cryptographic proof**.

Verification is foundational to the Satoshium philosophy.

---

## Cryptographic Governance
A governance model where **rules are enforced through code and cryptography**, rather than social or institutional trust alone.
