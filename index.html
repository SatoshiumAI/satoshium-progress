What Satoshium Is

Satoshium is a long-term platform for decentralized intelligence systems, cryptographic governance, trust and verification frameworks, simulation environments, and Bitcoin-aligned knowledge infrastructure.

In practical terms, Satoshium is being built as a layered ecosystem: foundational terminology, system specifications, intelligent interfaces, governance controls, experimental labs, public documentation, and a visible build record that ties the whole platform together.
